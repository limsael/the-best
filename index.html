A quiet beginning

A letter, just for you.

Some words are worth saying

For the days ahead.

May they be kinder. May they be brighter.
May they bring you back to yourself.

Take your time. There’s no rush.

There is no question here. Only a wish.

01
Chapter I 01 / 03

The wish

May tomorrow
be kinder.

More than anything, I hope the days ahead meet you gently — with the kind of rest that reaches deeper than sleep, and the quiet strength to feel like yourself again.

I hope recovery comes without hurry. That each morning carries a little less weight, and each evening leaves you with a little more peace.

May there be good news. Easy laughter. Food that tastes right again. Windows open to fresh air. All the ordinary things that become precious when they return.

You deserve time that asks nothing of you except to heal. Let the world wait while you do.

“Healing has its own weather. Let it change at its own pace.”

Chapter II 02 / 03

The unsaid

Some things deserve
better words.

II

There are words I spoke from anger and hurt that should never have been placed between us. They were unfair, and I am sorry for the harm they may have caused.

I won’t dress that apology in explanations or ask you to make it easier for me. I only want to name the mistake plainly: you deserved more care than I gave in those moments.

This is not an attempt to reopen what has already been difficult. It is simply the truth I should have offered with calmer hands, and better words.

No reply is needed.
No forgiveness is being asked for.

Let the difficult things rest here.
Chapter III 03 / 03

For whatever comes next

May life surprise you
in beautiful ways.

I hope you find your way toward days that feel open again — days full of health, purpose, small adventures, and reasons to look forward.

Whatever shape the future takes, I hope it is generous with you. I hope you keep the people who bring you peace close, and discover places that make the world feel new.

You have mattered in my life. That remains true without needing anything in return — not an answer, not a different ending, not even a place in what comes next.

Some chapters change us even after they close. I am grateful for what was good, and I am ready to leave the rest without bitterness.

Be well. Be happy. Be here for all the good that is still coming.

With care, always.
